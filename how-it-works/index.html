    How it works

    This page goes deep on purpose — it's the proof that the homepage summary is real and tested, not just copy. You don't need to follow all of it to use the result.

    The flow

    flowchart LR
  F[Form / Ad / WhatsApp<br/>new lead] -->|webhook + secret| S[Read Settings]
  S --> E[Read existing lead]
  E --> B[Build Response]
  B --> Q[Leads sheet<br/>add or update]
  Q --> C1{Has contact?}
  C1 -->|yes| R1[Reply to lead<br/>email/WhatsApp]
  Q --> C2{Owner contact?}
  C2 -->|yes| R2[Alert owner<br/>WhatsApp/email]
  M[Every morning] --> RD[Read Leads] --> RS[Read Settings] --> DG[Build digest] --> N2[WhatsApp + email to each owner]

    All the business rules live in one file, respond.js. build.js copies it into the workflow's Code nodes, so the rules that are tested are the same ones that run.

    After-hours rule

    Business hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 IST. The reply to the lead goes out immediately, any time — that's the entire point of the workflow. The after-hours check only adds a flag for the owner alert and the morning digest, so a human still follows up personally once the day starts, instead of the lead being forgotten.

    Flags

    A row is always written, even when something's wrong, and the problem is spelled out in the Flags column and the owner alert:

    Flag Meaning
    Missing: name, what they're asking about Required fields the intake source didn't send
    No phone or email to reply to Can't send an instant reply — flagged instead of silently failing
    Arrived after hours – needs personal follow-up Came in outside business hours; the auto-reply still went out
    No owner set for team Sales Nobody in Settings to alert

    When the same lead comes in again

    The workflow matches on the intake source's lead/response ID.

    • It updates the row rather than creating a duplicate.
    • The Status is never overwritten — a lead marked Contacted stays Contacted.
    • The reply still fires again on an update (e.g. the lead resubmitted with more detail), since that's cheap and better than silently skipping someone who tried again.

    Morning digest

    • Who it covers: leads received in the last 24 hours.
    • What it flags: anyone still New with an open flag — missing info, no contact method, or arrived after hours.
    • Why daily, not weekly: speed-to-lead is an hours-scale problem, not a days-scale one; a weekly cadence (like CloseLoop's) would be too slow here.

    What's real and what's sample

    • Real: the workflow, the rules, and their tests (node respond.test.js).
    • Sample:
    • Every lead is invented. send-test.sh posts a generic lead-shaped sample.
    • The field names in fromIntake() are a starting point for five common sources (see the mapping), not confirmed against any specific client's actual payload yet.
    • WhatsApp replies need a Meta-approved message template before the business can message a new contact first — that's Meta's rule, not a limitation of this workflow. Email is the reliable default until a template's approved.

    Files

    File What
    respond.js All rules: intake field mapping, after-hours check, flags, reply text, owner alert, morning digest
    respond.test.js Fixed-date checks for every rule (node respond.test.js)
    build.js Builds workflow.json with respond.js inlined into the Code nodes
    workflow.json Import into n8n (no credentials inside)
    payloads/, send-test.sh Sample leads and the script that sends them
