    Every morning: a quick digest flags anyone who still needs a personal follow-up.

    Often cited in this space: replying within 5 minutes is associated with a ~21x difference in qualifying a lead, versus replying later. No single named study backs the exact number, but it's the industry's own stated reason this matters.

    The problem this solves

    The business that replies first usually wins the deal — but most small teams check their leads whenever they get a free moment, which can be hours later. By then:

    • The lead has already messaged three other businesses and is talking to whoever answered first.
    • A Friday-evening or weekend inquiry sits untouched until Monday, by which point they've moved on.

    FirstReply replies the instant someone shows interest, any time of day, so no one's waiting on you — and a short morning digest makes sure a real person still follows up personally on anyone who needs it.
